# LeanOps-Manufacturing-Supply-Chain-Intelligence

## Project overview

An end-to-end manufacturing analytics solution designed to transform operational data into actionable insights across production performance, quality management, and supply chain efficiency.

This project demonstrates how manufacturing organizations can use data analytics to improve decision-making, identify operational risks, and optimize performance through interactive dashboards and analytical workflows.

---

# Business problem

Manufacturing operations generate large volumes of data across production lines, equipment performance, quality inspections, workforce activity, and supply chain processes. However, this data is often distributed across multiple systems, making it difficult for operational leaders to quickly identify performance issues and improvement opportunities.

This project addresses the challenge of creating a centralized analytics solution that provides visibility into key operational questions:

* Are production lines operating efficiently?
* Where are downtime and productivity losses occurring?
* Which factors contribute to quality risks?
* How effectively are suppliers and logistics processes performing?
* Which operational areas require immediate improvement attention?

---

# Business objective

The goal of this project is to build a manufacturing operations analytics framework that converts raw operational data into meaningful business intelligence.

The solution enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor production performance through key operational KPIs
* Identify efficiency gaps and downtime drivers
* Analyze quality trends and manufacturing risks
* Evaluate supply chain reliability
* Support continuous improvement initiatives through data-driven insights

---

# Solution delivered

The project combines:

* **Power BI** interactive dashboards for operational monitoring
* **Python analytics** for data exploration and predictive analysis
* **SQL workflows** for data preparation and transformation
* **Structured KPI frameworks** for manufacturing performance measurement

The final dashboard provides a centralized command view of manufacturing operations, allowing users to analyze production efficiency, quality performance, and supply chain health.

##  My findings from the analysis & recommendations

> **My analysis reveals that while macro plant performance looks exceptionally stable with a high 99.97% Asset Availability, hidden vulnerabilities exist in the logistical workflows where a 3.06-day average delivery delay is directly triggering localized material stockouts and inflating a £40.25M operating footprint.**

---

###  Strategic action plan for stakeholders

*   **Enforce procurement controls:** Immediately implement a strict **95% minimum floor** on supplier quality scores to audit and hold underperforming vendors before material shortages hit the production line.
*   **Cap floor bottlenecks:** Mandate a **2.5% maximum threshold** on Downtime Rates, transitioning engineering teams from reactive fixes to predictive maintenance intervals scheduled during lower labor-throughput windows.
*   **Unify plant floor decisions:** Deploy this live, cloud-hosted **4-Quadrant Command Center** onto operations tablets to synchronize communication across production, procurement, and logistics teams, preventing misaligned shift handovers.
